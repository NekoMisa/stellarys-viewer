FIRE KITTY POSER PROTOTYPE 0.1 — REVISION 2
Base: Firestorm 7.2.4 (80712), with Fire Kitty Fix 0.1.0 AMD changes.
Unofficial experimental Windows x64 / AVX2 build. Unsigned. Manual updates.

Extract the complete portable archive into its own folder. Run
Firestorm-FireKittyPoser.exe. Do not merge it into an installed viewer.
It uses a new FirestormFireKittyPoser_x64 settings and cache identity.
Your existing Firestorm, Fire Kitty Fix and Black Dragon profiles are separate.
No personal settings, accounts, credentials or cache are included.

This prototype extends Firestorm's existing poser. It is not a complete
Black Dragon feature port and does not add Black Dragon's renderer or UI.
Pose changes are LOCAL to the photographer's viewer. No pose sync is included.
The existing Fire Kitty Fix rendering changes are retained without edits.

MANUAL TEST (requires another consenting person)
1. Log in yourself. Open Avatar > Poser.
   Test your own pose, joint editing, Stop Posing and reopening the window.
2. Ask the other person to use Black Dragon with its poser permission protocol,
   or this prototype. Stock Firestorm does not implement this handshake and
   may display the request as a raw ;PoserRequest IM. The recipient must
   support the protocol; a missing response expires after two minutes.
3. Stand in the same region, within 50 metres. In the Poser's Model tab,
   refresh the list, select the other person, and click Ask to pose.
4. Before acceptance, Start Posing and joint editing must remain unavailable.
   Decline once and verify they stay unavailable. Requests are rate limited;
   wait at least 30 seconds before trying again.
5. Ask again and have the other person explicitly allow it. The status must
   change to Permission granted. Click Start Posing, then select a joint and
   change its rotation. Verify that only your view changes.
6. Test joint reset, T-pose, Stop Posing, starting again, and closing the poser.
   Closing the poser always stops local poses of other avatars.
7. While posing, have the other person revoke permission using Black Dragon's
   controls, this prototype's Revoke my permission button, or the exact IM
   command ;PoserDeny. The pose must stop and controls must become unavailable.
8. Test leaving the region, teleporting back and relogging: fresh permission
   must be required. Also test blocking/unblocking the other person.
9. Recheck the mesh that used to flicker and ordinary Firestorm photography,
   camera controls, toolbars and animesh posing.

Permission is independent in each direction. Revoke my permission withdraws
permission YOU gave the selected person to pose YOU. Stop Posing stops the
pose YOU are editing. This prototype accepts permission only after an active
request, with a two-minute response deadline. The legacy Black Dragon wire
protocol has no nonce; session/region checks and deadlines narrow its scope.

Build success and automated consent tests are not proof of live cross-viewer
compatibility or correct visual posing. See VALIDATION.md for checks actually
performed. Do not use this prototype as a replacement for a working viewer
until the manual tests pass.
